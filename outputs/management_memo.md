# Retail finance review

Synthetic case as of June 30, 2026. All amounts USD.

## Decision
Prioritize unit-cost management and working-capital discipline before committing to the upside demand case.

## Evidence
- 3,240 order records, 162 segment-months, three regions and three categories.
- Actual EBITDA less budget: $-319,865. Largest bridge driver: Variable cost.
- Base twelve-month revenue: $13,837,306; EBITDA: $2,224,187.
- Base ending cash: $2,296,729; downside ending cash: $1,004,736. No financing plug is applied.
- Holdout seasonal WAPE: 2.8%; trailing-mean benchmark: 25.3%. This synthetic result does not establish real-world forecasting accuracy.

## Proposed actions
Operations: investigate fulfillment unit costs before next monthly review. Commercial: validate price elasticity before a price increase. Treasury: evaluate inventory days and supplier terms together. Finance: rerun all cases after replacing assumptions with approved inputs. These are proposed case-study actions, not stakeholder approvals.

## Accounting and model boundaries
Net price includes modeled discounts/returns. Inventory/AP use merchandise COGS only; fulfillment is expensed separately within variable costs. This is an operating cash forecast, not a full GAAP balance sheet. Sales tax, debt, financing, deferred tax, and opening fixed-asset rollforwards are excluded. Cash flow deducts capex and working capital, adds back noncash depreciation through EBITDA, and taxes only positive EBIT. Opening AR/inventory/AP use stated 30-day proxies; forecast uses actual calendar days.
